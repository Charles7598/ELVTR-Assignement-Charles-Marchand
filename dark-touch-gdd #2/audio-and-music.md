# Dark Touch — Audio and Music

Part of the [Dark Touch GDD](GDD.md). Decisions only; open points are in [Open questions](open-questions.md).

## Music

- **Style:** dark neoclassical. **The cello is always the lead melody.** Mood reference: [Requiem for Wonderland by Nox Cantabile](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rF7QvXAZkVo).
- **Phonk elements to experiment with:** slowed phonk rhythms and deep bass under the strings. Reference: [Dark Phonk String Quartet Vol.22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xOWrHfuO_28), but with the cello kept as the lead melody. The best mix will be found by testing.
- **Adaptive layers:** the music changes with the situation: exploration, combat, boss.
- **Signature raise sound:** raising a soldier plays an iconic cello motif. It is a key part of the game's identity.
- **Production:** tracks are created with AI tools by the [Music Director agent](agents/music-director.md) and iterated with the designer until validated. References are mood boards only, never reused audio.

## Sound effects

- Sourced from free, **commercial-safe** sound banks only: CC0, Pixabay license, or CC-BY with credits.
- Found by the [Sound Designer agent](agents/sound-designer.md) and reviewed on the [Dark Touch Sound Board](https://claude.ai/artifact/RfTmdaNEp5cF3bLusSwN8b): 3 candidates per sound, pick one or ask for 3 more.
- How each action sounds and feels together with its visuals is specified by the [Game Feel Director](agents/game-feel-director.md).
