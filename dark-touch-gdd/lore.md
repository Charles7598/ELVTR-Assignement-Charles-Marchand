# Dark Touch — Lore

Part of the [Dark Touch GDD](GDD.md). Simple for now; to be expanded later.

## The world

**Cel'ardor** (working name). The whole continent is at war. In Cel'ardor, mages need both arms to cast spells.

## The story

You are a mage who lost an arm. Believing you would never cast a spell again, you accepted a pact with a dark entity you know little about. It gave you a magic arm that casts spells on its own, granting you the powers of a necromancer.

You wake up in a small town. To protect the village, you raid the enemy army before it can attack. You know you must relearn magic entirely with this new power, so you are glad to start by protecting a small village, and later bring destruction to your enemies once you have mastered it.
