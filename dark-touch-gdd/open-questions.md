# Dark Touch — Open Questions

Decisions still to make for the [Dark Touch GDD](GDD.md). Tick an item when it is decided, then move the decision into the GDD or its companion documents.

## Combat and abilities

- [ ] Final names of the abilities (except Unholy Impale).
- [ ] The two variants of each ability.
- [ ] Whether aimed abilities get a soft-targeting assist or stay raw aim (decided during prototyping).
- [ ] Which abilities combo with each other.
- [ ] Which abilities, if any, can be cast while moving.
- [ ] How Spirit regenerates or is gained.
- [ ] What causes stuns, for the player and for enemies.
- [ ] Rounding direction for damage: to the nearest whole number, or always down?

## Army and soldiers

- [ ] Player abilities granted by Mages and Rogues.
- [ ] When a type's ability is available: with at least one soldier of that type, always, or something else.
- [ ] How soldier-granted abilities fit on the action bar.
- [ ] Grade and rank odds per difficulty tier.
- [ ] Does a raised elite keep its aura and stat boost?
- [ ] Do raised soldiers that die during a run stay dead until the run ends?
- [ ] How the 10 army slots unlock over time.
- [ ] The exact level rule for raising a mob.
- [ ] The list of orders players can give, and which spells direct soldiers.
- [ ] Whether stances or behaviours are set per command group.
- [ ] How starting soldiers are chosen before a run, from the soldiers kept.

## Enemies and level

- [ ] Enemy roster for the level: which types appear, how often, how many elites.
- [ ] The boss of the level: design and mechanics.
- [ ] Level layout: the mix of rooms and open areas, hidden chest placement, run length for the one-level demo.
- [ ] The size and source of the random name list.

## Progression

- [ ] Talismans: stats they boost, rarity, drop rates, how many can be owned, whether found ones are kept after a death.
- [ ] Experience and levels: what levelling gives during a run and across runs.
- [ ] The level-up choice during a run: what is offered.
- [ ] The talent list, including talents that change how Souls are gained.
- [ ] Which variants unlock at which level.
- [ ] Difficulty tiers: how many, how each is unlocked, how much stats and grade odds scale.

## Presentation

- [ ] Look of the dark-magic arm effect.
- [ ] Is the rank aura visible on living mobs, or only on corpses and soldiers? How do elites look now that auras show rank?
- [ ] Music: how much phonk to blend in, and in which layers.
- [ ] UI: screen order, and the UI Designer's model.
- [ ] Default assignment of the 14 key slots between spells, command groups and orders.
- [ ] Accessibility options, such as reducing camera shake.
- [ ] Lore beyond the opening story: the entity, the war, the factions.

## Balance

- [ ] All numbers: damage, HP, cooldowns, cast times, Spirit costs, the revive cost, Reap buff values, talisman boosts.
